Native iOS mail client

Your inbox,
read by your iPhone.

OrbMail groups your mail into living clusters, writes you a daily briefing and drafts your replies — using Apple Intelligence, on the device. Nothing is uploaded. There is no server to upload it to.

Get OrbMail

iOS 18 or later. AI features require an iPhone with Apple Intelligence.

What it does

Clusters, not folders

Mail organises itself into groups that reflect how you actually live — work, banking, travel, school, deliveries — and the Orb shows the shape of your week at a glance.

A daily briefing

Every morning OrbMail writes an edition of your inbox: what happened, what is waiting on you, what can wait. Written on your phone, in your language.

Replies, drafted

Summaries and reply suggestions that sound like you, generated locally. You always approve before anything is sent.

Any mailbox

Gmail, iCloud, Outlook, Yahoo, Proton Bridge and any IMAP provider — over 200 providers configure themselves from your address.

Where your mail goes

Nowhere. That is the whole design.

No servers

OrbMail has no backend. Your iPhone connects directly to your mail provider over an encrypted IMAP and SMTP connection, the same way Apple Mail does.

No accounts

There is nothing to sign up for. No OrbMail account, no profile, no cloud sync.

No third parties

No analytics, no advertising, no trackers. The AI runs on the device through Apple Intelligence — your mail is never sent to any AI service.
